## 7. Current Website UX, Technical & SEO Audit

### 7.1 Critical UX & Usability Failures
* **Template Mismatch (E-Commerce Storefront on an Education Institute):**
  * The current website runs on a generic Justdial Omni e-commerce template featuring a shopping cart (`0 Cart`), "Buy at Best Prices", "Add to Cart", "Filter by Price", and "Session / Hour" pricing models. This drastically harms the academic credibility of a serious tech institute.
* **Unedited Placeholder Text:**
  * The SAP page literally displays raw template instructions: `"click to edit text click to edit text click to edit text"`.
  * The Gallery page displays: `"This content will display your pictures WORKSHOP"`.
  * The Akida page displays: `"Click here to edit the text. It's easy. Just click here to add your own content and make changes to it."`.
* **Broken Navigation & Information Architecture:**
  * No structured dropdown menu for course tracks.
  * Essential pages (About Us, Contact Us, Individual Courses) route back to the homepage or obscure hashed URLs (`/gallery/page-57472755/?scrollTo=877667516`).
* **Heading Hierarchy & Visual Clutter:**
  * The `<h1>` tag on the homepage is an entire unformatted 80-word paragraph jammed with keywords, followed by a second `<h1>` containing raw phone numbers (`<h1>+91-6266846474+91-7000882359|+91-8103168797</h1>`).
* **Lack of Visual Design System:**
  * Inconsistent typography, low-contrast text, missing responsive padding, and no cohesive brand color palette.

### 7.2 Technical & SEO Deficiencies
* **Meta Title Issues:** Homepage title is simply `"Data Science course in Raipur"`, completely ignoring the institute name and broader course catalog.
* **Aggressive Keyword Stuffing:** Raw meta keywords list 15 variations of `"training in Raipur"`, which search engines penalize.
* **Robots.txt & Sitemap Conflicts:** `robots.txt` disallows multiple crawler user agents (`dotbot`, `AhrefsBot`, `SemrushBot`, `CuteStat`) and exposes auto-generated cart URLs.
* **Mobile Viewport Bottlenecks:** Uses `user-scalable=no` in viewport meta tag, violating modern WCAG accessibility guidelines.
* **Security & Header Standards:** Missing structured schema (`EducationalOrganization`, `Course`, `LocalBusiness`), OpenGraph tags point to arbitrary Justdial banners.
